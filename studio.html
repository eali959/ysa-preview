An obsession with design.

A Toronto architecture and interior design studio with one stubborn belief: a building should be beautiful, perform brilliantly, and earn its owner every dollar the land will allow.

YawarSiddiqui

Founder and principal architect, OAA

16+years of turning tight sites and difficult by-laws into opportunities

Yawar’s practice is defined by a meticulous eye and a refusal to settle for “good enough for permit”. He has designed for multi-national brands like KFC, Taco Bell, Pizza Hut and Hard Rock Cafe, and for first-time developers who had never navigated an approval in their lives. The same care goes into both.

His conviction: architecture is not about constructing buildings, but about creating meaningful, timeless spaces that enrich the lives, and the balance sheets, of the people who inhabit them.

Every building begins as a single line.

It still starts with a pencil.

Before a line is drawn in software, ideas are tested by hand. It keeps the thinking honest and the details considered.

What we believe.

Maximum yield, always
We interrogate the zoning by-law for every unit and every square foot, then pursue the amendments to unlock more. On one recent site, that meant 40 extra units.
Beautiful and functional
Most firms design to clear the permit. We curate something considered and genuinely beautiful, because it lasts longer, leases faster and adds to the street around it.
With you, start to finish
From the first zoning review to the day the Chief Building Official grants occupancy, we are hands-on, especially for clients doing this for the first time.

Accredited, and trusted by names you know.
