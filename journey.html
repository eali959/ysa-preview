The nine stages, at a glance.

  1. Property and zoning review

    It begins with your land, vacant or already built. We open the file with a zoning review: what the by-law permits, what it does not, and where the hidden yield is. This is where most of your profit is won or lost.

    YS Architects Inc., due diligence
    About 1.5 months

  2. Zoning amendment and by-law approvals

    If the zoning already allows your vision, we skip ahead. If not, we take it on: a zoning by-law amendment, an official plan amendment or a minor variance, pushing the envelope to unlock the density your land deserves.

    City and Committee of Adjustment
    About 9 months

  3. Site plan approval

    We prepare and submit the site plan package: siting, massing, servicing, landscaping, streetscape. A design that fits the community and passes the desk of every reviewer.

    Municipal planning department
    About 8 months

  4. Building permit

    Full construction documents, stamped and submitted under the Ontario Building Code, coordinated across every engineering discipline so the permit is issued, not returned.

    Ontario Building Code
    About 3 months

  5. Construction and general review

    The building rises. We are on site performing general review, verifying that the work conforms to the drawings, the code and our design standards, floor by floor and detail by detail.

    YS Architects Inc., on site
    About 18 months

  6. Final inspection and commissioning

    As construction completes, we carry out the final inspection and commission the building’s systems, confirming everything performs as designed before anyone moves in.

    Architect and engineers
    About 2 months

  7. Completion letter

    The architect and engineers of record issue the completion letter: the statement that the building meets the code and the by-laws, and is ready for occupancy.

    Architect and engineers of record
    About 1 month

  8. Permit closure and occupancy

    The Chief Building Official reviews our letter and the inspectors’ records, then closes the permit and grants occupancy. The keys are yours.

    Chief Building Official
    About 1.5 months

  9. Occupancy and revenue

    You occupy or lease the space and it starts working for you, month after month. A building that fits its community and maximises your return. That is the whole point.

    You, the owner
    About 3 months
