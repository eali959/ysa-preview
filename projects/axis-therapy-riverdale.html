Axis Therapy, Riverdale

Toronto, Ontario, 2022. Move to look around, scroll to walk in.Scroll to walk in.

3,400square feet

A clinic that feels nothing like a clinic. Warm wood, soft black lines, and curves where you expect corners.

Axis came to us for their flagship physiotherapy and performance space. The brief was equal parts clinical and hospitable: it had to work for athletes recovering under load and for first-time patients nervous about walking in.

We answered with a restrained palette of natural wood, black detailing and rounded columns, and a plan that reads as one continuous space while quietly separating reception, treatment and wellness.
