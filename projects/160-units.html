We turned 120 units into 160.

A developer came to us with a scheme drawn by a previous architect. When we ran the analysis, it did not even conform to the zoning by-law. So we started again.

Forty extra units are forty extra streams of revenue, month after month, for the life of the building.

Yield is a matter of design, not luck.

  1. Re-read the by-law

    We went back to the zoning by-law line by line, setbacks, angular planes, parking and amenity, and mapped the true buildable envelope the previous scheme had missed.

  2. Re-plan the massing

    With the real envelope in hand, we reworked the massing and unit mix so every conforming square foot did work, without fighting the site’s tight geometry.

  3. Prove it conforms

    The new scheme was tested against the by-law before anyone celebrated. 160 units that pass, not 120 that never would have.

“The site never changed. The thinking did.”
