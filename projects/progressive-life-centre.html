Progressive Life Centre

Ajax, Ontario, 2024. Move to look around, scroll to walk in.Scroll to walk in.

8,710square feet

A building designed to meet people where they are.

Every room was shaped around how its occupants experience space. Therapy rooms and physical-development areas sit beside sensory-friendly retreats calibrated for light, sound and texture, so the environment supports regulation instead of overwhelming it.

The centrepiece is a suite of real-world life-skills spaces, rehearsal rooms for independence: a mock kitchen, a classroom, even a small grocery and coffee shop. Barrier-free wayfinding threads it together, so the building is intuitive for every visitor.
