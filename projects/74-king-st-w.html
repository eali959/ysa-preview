Projects / Multi-unit residential

74 King St. W

Bowmanville, Ontario, 2022. Move to look around, scroll to walk in.Scroll to walk in.

8,221square feet

Two tired units on a heritage main street, reborn as ten without erasing the history.

The two-storey building had good bones and a protected façade. Instead of fighting the heritage designation we worked with it: renovating the ground and second floors and adding a set-back third storey that reads as new but defers to the original street wall.

The result multiplies the rentable units five-fold while the streetscape keeps its character, with standing-seam metal roofing and energy-efficient glazing behind a preserved brick face.
